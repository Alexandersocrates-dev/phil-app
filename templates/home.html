What Phil gives you

Scales from one school to a trust

Safeguarding & trust

Built to sit alongside your safeguarding procedures, not replace them

Phil is a session-recording and course-delivery tool, not a safeguarding reporting system. Where its role ends and your own procedure begins is set out plainly, so nobody has to guess.

A mandatory step, every session

Every mentor completes a safeguarding flag and a factual note on every session, whether they have been mentoring for a term or a decade.

Never a substitute for your DSL

Phil doesn’t send alerts, notify a DSL, or monitor content. Mentors report concerns through the school’s normal route, the same day, exactly as KCSIE requires.

Clear, reviewable access

Admin accounts can look up any pupil’s records, including safeguarding notes. A ready-made policy template sets out exactly who sees what.
