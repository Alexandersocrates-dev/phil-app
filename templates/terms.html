Why this matters

Named terms, honest figures

Phil can’t know your term dates. Without them the impact report offers a rough window and calls it “this term”, which is the figure a head would quote to governors.

Once your terms are here, each one becomes its own button on the impact report, and the report header states the exact dates it covers.

Changing a term later doesn’t alter anything already recorded — reports read the dates when they run.
